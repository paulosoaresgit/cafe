Experience the ultimate coffeehouse performance at home with the Ninja Luxe Café Premier. Whether you're craving a rich espresso, a classic filter coffee, or an indulgent cappuccino, this 3-in-1 machine delivers perfection with every cup.

The built-in conical burr grinder ensures your beans are ground fresh just moments before extraction, capturing the peak flavour and aroma. With 19 bars of pressure, the espresso extraction is smooth, rich, and topped with a beautiful crema.

Forget complicated milk texturing — our auto-clean frother creates perfectly silky microfoam at the touch of a button, for dairy and plant-based milks alike. Enjoy your favourite specialty drinks without the hassle of a traditional steam wand.
